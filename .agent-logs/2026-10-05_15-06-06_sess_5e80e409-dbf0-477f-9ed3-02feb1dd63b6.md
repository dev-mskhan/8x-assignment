CREATE EXTENSION IF NOT EXISTS vector;

That's required before the embedding column works.

User Query

    ↓

Normal SQL Filters

    ↓

Ranking

    ↓

AI Response

For your example:

"gaming laptops under 200k with RTX"

Do not rely only on vector similarity.

Use:

Semantic search

+

price <= 200000

+

category = laptop

+

GPU contains RTX

That gives you much more reliable product search.
